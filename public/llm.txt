# Light As Air — Official Godox KNOWLED Dealer & Cinematic Lighting

> Light As Air is the premier UK specialist for professional film, video, and TV studio lighting equipment, featuring Godox KNOWLED series, LED fixtures, rental packages, sales, and live equipment auctions.

- **Official Godox KNOWLED Dealer**: Authorized distribution and support for high-end Godox cinematic lighting fixtures.

- **Equipment Purchasing**: Direct sales of new and certified pre-owned film production gear.
- **Live Auctions**: Bidding platform for high-value studio equipment and production lots.
- **Equipment Requests**: Custom quotes for full crew and production lighting packages.
